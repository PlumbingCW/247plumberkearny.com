Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call, email or visit this website.

Information we receive

When you call or email, we receive the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the plumbing problem. We use it to schedule and perform the work and to follow up. This website has no forms.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use what you share to respond to your request, to dispatch a crew and to improve our service. We do not sell your information.
