Winter freeze and spring thaw in Belleville

Water in a pipe expands as it freezes, and a pipe in an unheated attic, garage or exterior wall can split when temperatures stay below 32 degrees for hours. The break often shows up when the pipe thaws and water starts flowing, usually while no one is home to see it.

If a faucet runs slow in a cold spell, open it a little and warm the pipe with a hair dryer set low, moving along the line toward the faucet. Never use a flame. If you see a bulge or a split, shut off the main valve and call the 24/7 line before the thaw lets water flow.

Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Belleville

Whether the break came from a freeze or from age, these four steps limit the damage.

Shut off the main valve

Find the main where the water line enters the building. If the main is stuck or leaks at the stem, call us and the Belleville Water Department at (973) 450-3414 about the curb valve.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for circuits that run through wet walls or ceilings, and stay away from sagging ceilings that hold water.

Clear the area

Move belongings off the floor, place towels or a bucket and keep people out of rooms with a bulging ceiling.

Call the 24/7 line
